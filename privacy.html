The short version

I don't want your data. I don't need it, I don't want to track you, and I certainly don't want to sell it.

My approach to privacy is simple: collect as little as possible, ideally nothing at all.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to digital projects developed and published by Edd Hurst, including websites, web applications, Android applications, software, utilities and experiments.

This includes applications published on Google Play under the Edd Hurst developer name.

If a particular project has additional privacy requirements or handles information differently, those details will be clearly explained within that project.

2. Information collection

My projects are designed to work without collecting personal information.

I do not deliberately collect, request or store personal information for analytics, advertising, profiling or tracking purposes.

I do not use:

  • Advertising trackers.
  • Analytics services.
  • Tracking pixels.
  • Browser fingerprinting.
  • Cross-site tracking.
  • Advertising identifiers for profiling.

Where an application stores information to provide its functionality, this should be kept locally on your device wherever practical.

I do not sell personal information. I have no interest in doing so.

3. Cookies and tracking

My websites do not use tracking cookies or similar technologies to monitor your activity.

My applications do not include advertising or behavioural tracking.

Some third-party platforms and infrastructure providers may use their own technologies as part of providing their services. These are governed by their respective privacy policies.

4. Third-party services

Some projects are distributed or hosted through third-party platforms, including Google Play and GitHub Pages.

These services may independently process technical information, such as IP addresses, device information or server request logs, as part of operating their infrastructure and providing their services.

I do not use these services to build advertising profiles or track people across projects.

If a project integrates an external service or third-party software that processes personal information, its use will be disclosed in the relevant project documentation or within the application.

5. Data storage and security

I do not operate a central user database or maintain user profiles across my projects.

Where project functionality requires information to be stored locally on a device, that information remains under the control of the device and its operating system.

I do not retain personal information for advertising, analytics or profiling purposes.

6. Sharing and selling information

I do not sell, rent or trade personal information.

I do not share personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

Where third-party infrastructure is necessary to distribute or operate a project, that provider may process technical information under its own terms and privacy practices.

7. Your choices

You are free to stop using any of my projects at any time.

Where an application stores information locally, uninstalling the application or clearing its data will generally remove that locally stored information, subject to the behaviour of your device and any operating system backups.

8. Changes to this policy

If the way a project handles personal information changes, this policy will be updated to reflect those changes.

Any significant changes will be reflected in the relevant project documentation or application.

If you have any questions about privacy or how a particular project works, you can contact Edd Hurst using the following details.
